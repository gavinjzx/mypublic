PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: 2026-11-11

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application American Mahjong Score Keeper (package name: com.mahjtools.americanscore) published on the Google Play Store.

1. DATA COLLECTION

All game data you create in this app — game records, player names, scores, and any mahjong card data you enter by yourself — is stored only on your device. We do not operate any servers, and your data is never uploaded to us or to any remote server.

We do not collect, store, upload or share any personal identifiable information, including your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or files.

Card data you enter comes from the card you own. It stays on your device only and is never transmitted by the app.

This app displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

To serve and measure advertising, Google and its partners may automatically collect and process certain information, such as:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Your interaction with ads shown in the app

This data is collected and used by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising at any time via Android Settings → Google → Ads. The app remains fully functional with ads only; no payment or login is required.

3. PERMISSIONS

The app requests only the INTERNET permission, used solely to load advertisements. All scoring and game features work completely offline.

4. CHILDREN

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

5. USER RIGHTS

No account or registration is required. All locally stored data belongs to you. Uninstalling the app permanently and completely deletes all data stored on your device. To limit advertising data collection, use the Android advertising settings described in Section 2.
